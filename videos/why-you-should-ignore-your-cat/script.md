## HOOK
There's a weird thing that happens at almost every party with a cat.

The cat lover walks in, gets down on the floor, reaches out and says "pspspsps"… and the cat runs away.

Then the one guest who says "I'm not really a cat person" sits down on the couch… and the cat climbs straight into their lap.

That's not bad luck. It's the cat telling you exactly how it wants to be loved.

## WHY CHASING DOESN'T WORK
To a cat, a big animal walking straight at it, staring and reaching out with a giant hand, doesn't look friendly. It looks like something closing in.

Cats are hunters, but they're also small enough to be hunted. So what they care about most is control: being able to choose when to come closer and when to leave.

## WHAT THE SCIENCE SAYS
In 2021, researchers tested this. They gave people simple rules for meeting cats: let the cat choose, let the cat come to you, and keep petting short and gentle.

When people followed those rules, the cats got friendlier. They rubbed, purred and stayed longer, and they swatted and hissed less.

## IT'S NOT THAT CATS DON'T CARE
And no, cats aren't cold. In one study, most cats picked time with a person over food. Another found that most cats bond with their owners the way babies bond with their parents.

They want you. They just want to be the one who decides.

## HOW TO "IGNORE" YOUR CAT THE RIGHT WAY
So here's the trick. Sit down. Don't stare. Hold out one finger at nose height, and wait.

If your cat sniffs it and rubs its cheek against it, that's a yes. Pet the cheeks and chin, stop after a few strokes, and let it ask for more.

And if your cat looks at you… you already know what to do. Blink slowly.

## OUTRO
Because with cats, the less you chase, the more you get.
