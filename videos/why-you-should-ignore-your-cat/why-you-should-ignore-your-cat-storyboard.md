# Why You Should Ignore Your Cat — Storyboard

**Production Profile**
- Format: faceless animated explainer with no host. Story-driven hook, then explainer beats. It's a sequel to "Why You Should Blink Slowly At Your Cat" and ends on a slow-blink callback.
- Canvas: 1920x1080 (landscape, about 2:00)
- Art system: cute 2D cartoon in the same style as the slow-blink video, with soft rounded shapes, thick outlines and big expressive eyes. Subjects are AI-generated cutouts with the background removed, set on simple drawn backgrounds. Palette: warm cream, soft peach, sage green, dusty blue, with one pop color (coral red) for emphasis words. *(If the slow-blink video used a different palette or character design, match that instead. Consistency is what turns this into a series.)*
- Recurring elements:
  - **Mochi**: the cat from the slow-blink video. Placeholder name in this board; use the real character. Round, fluffy, huge eyes. Gets a new pose for every action.
  - **Cat Lover**: an over-excited guest in a cat-ear headband and a cat-print sweater, holding a treat bag. Always enters from the **left**.
  - **Not-a-Cat-Person**: a calm guest in a plain grey hoodie with a coffee mug, scrolling their phone. Always sits on the **right** end of the couch.
  - **Owner**: the viewer stand-in for the how-to section. Use the human from the slow-blink video if there was one.
  - **Party Room**: a living-room background with a couch on the right, rug in the center, string lights and a snack table on the left.
  - **Cat-Vision filter**: a slightly fish-eye, desaturated, vignetted cat's-eye view, used to show the world as Mochi sees it.
- Pacing: 37 scenes over about 300 words, about 2:00. The average scene is about 3.2 s; the hook scenes are faster.

Scene 1 — The hook: party setup

* VO SEGMENT: "There's a weird thing that happens at almost every party with a cat."
* CORE IDEA: Set up a familiar, cozy scene with a cat in the middle of a party.
* SCENE CONCEPT: A busy living-room party. Mochi sits loaf-style in the center of the rug like the guest of honor, slowly turning its head to watch the guests.
* ASSETS NEEDED: Party Room (bg), Mochi (ai-cutout: loaf pose on rug, calm half-lidded eyes, head turning), 3–4 background party guests (ai-cutout, simple, low detail, chatting with cups)
* LAYOUT: Illustration full-frame. Mochi at the center about 25% width on the rug; guests as smaller silhouettes at the left and right edges in the mid-ground. Mochi is in front and nothing overlaps its face.
* VISUAL BUILD: Room and guests from start. Mochi's head turns on "weird thing", and a tiny "?" pops over Mochi on "party with a cat."
* TEXT: —
* SFX: soft party murmur and clinking glasses under the whole scene; tiny "bloop" on the "?"
* MOTION NOTE: slow push-in toward Mochi across the scene.
* LAYOUT VERDICT: OK — an establishing frame. Its job is to make you go "aww" fast, so keep it short.
